Chapter II The Secret Code

Why She Was Crowned "Mrs. Cupcake"

People often ask, "Why does Arnab call his Didi 'Mrs. Cupcake'?" Well, look closely at a cupcake: it's sweet, colourful, brings instant happiness to everyone who sees it, and has just that irresistible sprinkle of drama and sweetness on top!

Whether she's scolding Arnab for staying up late or secretly slipping him extra treats, she is forever the sweet confectionery heart of our home.

Chapter III Festive Glory

Durga Puja, Colors & Bengali Elegance

Nothing compares to the joyous festival days. With Didi adorned in her gorgeous vibrant yellow saree and Arnab in his royal purple kurta, celebrating Bijoya, Sindoor Khela, and festive nights.

Amidst the dhol beats, the dhunuchi dances, and the abir colors on our foreheads, those candid smiles capture what family truly means. Didi isn't just an elder sister; she's the festive light of our celebrations!

My Dearest Mrittica Didi,

Turning 24 is a milestone, but to me, you will always be the same sweet, caring, and slightly dramatic elder sister who has been my rock through every phase of growing up.

I gave you the nickname "Mrs. Cupcake" not just because you love sweetness, but because you make every ordinary day feel like a celebration. Whether we are dressing up for Durga Puja, traveling to hill towns, sharing funny selfies, or arguing over the most trivial things—having you as my Didi is the greatest privilege and blessing life could have given me.

As you step forward in life, remember: "There is a future version, don't look back." May your days be filled with endless laughter, boundless success, good health, and all the cupcakes in the world. No matter how old we get, I will always be right here cheering for you, annoying you, and protecting you just like you have always protected me.

With all my love and blessings,

Your Little Bro, Arnab ❤️

Forever & Always
